## Canon decision

The Galvanoscopio is not invented by Maese Lumen. It is a functional diagnostic instrument inherited from an older, technically more complete Ohmdal. Lumen can maintain, repair and use it in practice, but does not fully understand its theory, scales or all operating modes. His explanations may mix empirical knowledge with superstition. The instrument itself remains governed by real electrical behavior; belief or ritual does not change its measurements.
