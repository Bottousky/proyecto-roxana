# Ohmdal Arco I — Area Reference Plan

## Purpose

Define conceptual direction for each authored area without overriding the accepted greybox topology or canon. These are production references: visual identity, landmarks, material hierarchy, before/after state and shot intent.

Global visual authority remains `OHMDAL_VISUAL_MATERIAL_BIBLE.md`.

Global material language:

- pale eroded stone;
- aged/oxidized copper, never passive neon;
- ceramic insulators;
- technical/workshop wood where justified;
- instrument glass;
- dark steel for mechanisms;
- still or moving water according to system state;
- dusty warm afternoon / restrained atmosphere.

Historical HD-2D references may inform mood or landmark intent only. They are not spatial authority for the PlayCanvas runtime.

## 1. Plaza — civic electrical hub

**Role:** orientation, memory and visible system state.

**Identity:** an old technical civic square whose infrastructure is integrated into architecture rather than pasted on. It is detained, not destroyed. The existing accepted Plaza is the baseline and should change minimally.

**Must preserve:** Portal Ω, Ohm landmark, Taller relationship, route to Omega/Castle, accepted composition.

**Before/after:** restoration is conveyed by service returning — bell mechanism, moving/working elements, water/activity, subtle practical light — not by recoloring the whole city.

**Reference cues:** old civic substation, public utility architecture, stone plaza with exposed but orderly conductors, ceramic standoffs.

## 2. Taller de Lumen — diagnostic atelier

**Role:** first real instrument/workbench culture.

**Identity:** practical electronics workshop inherited from a more technically capable past. Lumen maintains and interprets tools without being a caricature or omniscient engineer.

**Landmarks:** Galvanoscopio bench, tool storage, repair traces, local conductors, ceramic/glass/wood hierarchy.

**Mood:** close, warm, tactile, technical; less monumental than Plaza.

**Avoid:** generic alchemist lab, fantasy potions, random brass clutter.

## 3. Manantial / Central Hidroeléctrica — hero environment

**Role:** source of restored power and the strongest physical cause/effect chapter environment.

**Identity:** a hydroelectric headworks carved into pale stone, where the spring/lake feed has been engineered into channels, sluice, turbine/generator and high-current distribution. Ancient but credible, maintained in fragments, not a modern concrete dam.

**Landmarks:** intake/sluice gate, visible water channel, turbine/generator body, return/ground path, protection assembly, large ceramic insulators, busbars, measurement platform.

**Dormant state:** water unnaturally still or held; machinery inert; no decorative glow; ambient sound sparse.

**Restored state:** water moves, turbine/generator motion starts, contacts/mechanisms respond, localized event emission, spray/mist and low mechanical hum. The electrical path should be visually traceable from generator to outgoing conductors.

**Avoid:** fantasy waterfall temple, giant blue glow, cyberpunk power plant, generic steampunk pipes everywhere.

## 4. Castillo de la Red — civic distribution stronghold

**Role:** make series/parallel/distribution/protection spatially legible.

**Identity:** castle in the civic/defensive sense, but functionally a monumental distribution hall/substation. Architecture protects and organizes the network; it is not primarily a throne room.

**Landmarks:** central bus, branching galleries, isolators, protected service branches, visible seals/lockouts, maintenance balconies/panels.

**Spatial read:** player should see that one source feeds several services and that branches can be isolated without killing everything.

**Close-up allowance:** dense panels may use diegetic maintenance views anchored to real objects.

**Avoid:** generic medieval castle interiors, arbitrary puzzle-board wall detached from world state.

## 5. Forja — concentrated thermal/industrial demand

**Role:** embody power, current, heat, conductor limits and protection.

**Identity:** a functional forge/production space where electrical energy becomes useful heat and motion. Heavy load should feel physically demanding on the network.

**Landmarks:** furnace/load, thick conductors, protection/fuse assembly, temperature evidence, work path, material stock.

**Readability:** overload should communicate via heat, sound, protection response and service degradation, not a red UI bar alone.

**Avoid:** magical forge, lava cavern, endless sparks unrelated to electrical state.

## 6. Terrazas — distributed irrigation service

**Role:** show the social/service cost of allocating finite power.

**Identity:** stone agricultural/water terraces fed by powered pumps/gates. Visually calmer and more open than Forja; the contrast makes the trade-off understandable.

**Landmarks:** stepped water channels, pump/valve stations, distribution conductors, visible dry/wet or low/high-service states.

**Readability:** player can see which terraces receive water and how network decisions propagate spatially.

**Avoid:** generic farm decoration disconnected from power flow.

## 7. Faro / Lago — remote calibration culmination

**Role:** final technical synthesis and quiet narrative culmination.

**Identity:** austere technical lighthouse/measurement landmark over the lake, connected back to the network by a legible energy path. It should feel remote, precise and contemplative after denser industrial zones.

**Landmarks:** lighthouse mechanism/light, calibration panel, lake reference, outgoing/return conductors, Nereo's observation position when/if represented.

**Current gameplay truth:** DC calibration and stable samples. Do not visually imply an RC/transient mechanic that is not ratified.

**Avoid:** coastal fantasy beacon clichés, giant magical laser, unexplained pulse timing.

## 8. Final return — the same world, understood

**Role:** prove restoration and knowledge transfer through backtracking.

**Identity:** no new region language. The impact comes from revisiting the same systems now working: water, bell, services, machinery and modest civic activity.

**Rule:** final-state differences should be functional and local. Preserve stone/copper material identity; do not turn the world into a glowing victory skin.

## Hero/reference priorities

Highest reference-gate priority before final asset authoring:

2. Castle main distribution hall / central bus landmark.

4. Lighthouse mechanism/calibration landmark.

Generic support architecture can use authored procedural/Blender forms and verified reusable assets without a hero gate, provided they respect provenance and visual bible.
