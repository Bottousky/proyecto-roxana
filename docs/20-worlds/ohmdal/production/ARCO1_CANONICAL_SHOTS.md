# Ohmdal Arco I — Canonical Shots

These are the authored-pass comparison views. They are not cinematic targets; they are stable QA/readability views tied to actual player states.

## Capture rules

- same deterministic seed when applicable;
- desktop primary unless noted;
- main milestones also captured on mobile/touch;
- record renderer, `softwareRendered`, draw calls, triangles, active zones, transfer, lights/shadows and console/page errors;
- keep a no-post diagnostic for each authored stage's principal shot;
- camera may be tuned for readability but must not hide gameplay-critical geometry;
- captures are evidence, not permission to alter validated topology.
