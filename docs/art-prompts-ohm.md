## Atlas de animación

Referencia de estilo: `public/assets/characters.png`. Se reemplaza la identidad anterior de Ohm; los otros personajes conservan su atlas.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite sheet for the original HD-2D fantasy electronics game Ohmdal.
Create ONLY the newly redesigned companion OHM. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: match the hand-painted crisp JRPG sprite rendering of the human characters. Do NOT repeat any humans, and do NOT copy the humanoid robot design in its last row.
OHM DESIGN: a very simple, charming, compact old brass measuring instrument come alive. ONE single rounded oval/capsule chassis (slightly wider than tall), ONE large circular inset turquoise glass eye on the front, dark teal lower rim, a small recessed omega mark below the eye, two short broad integral brass skids/feet supporting a flat stable underside. No separate head or torso. No arms, no hands, no fingers, no legs, no antenna, no scarf, no backpack, no dangling cables. Print-friendly chunky silhouette that could later become a small 3D printed figurine. Minimal broad seams and a few large shallow screw heads, almost no tiny surface greebles. The eye communicates curious deadpan intelligence. Warm worn brass, dark teal, cyan glass.
EXACT SHEET GEOMETRY: landscape 1536x1024 canvas, SIX equal columns and FOUR equal rows, 24 isolated full-body sprites, equal 256x256 cells. Each sprite entirely inside its own cell with generous clear margins, same scale and baseline. Row1: facing down/toward camera, six gentle shuffling/waddling movement phases. Row2: facing screen-right, six phases. Row3: facing up/away, six phases, back has no eye. Row4: facing screen-left, six phases. Elevated classic RPG camera, about 25 degrees down, all views share this elevation. Stable oval body proportions, no morphing across frames. Movement is a tiny alternating skid shuffle and slight tilt, never humanoid walking.
Background: pure flat chroma key magenta #ff00ff. No shadows outside sprites. No floor, scenery, labels, grid lines, text, frame numbers or UI. Center the character consistently in every cell. Beautiful restrained sprite painting, simple readable shape, crisp dark contours.
