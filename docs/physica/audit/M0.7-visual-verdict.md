# Visual Director — M0.7 verdict (re-audit)

> Re-audit of `docs/physica/screenshots/shot-*.png` (11 shots, 7 scenes)
> after the **M0.7 fix batch** applied on top of the M0.6 hybrid art slice.
> References: Planet of Lana, Trine 4, INSIDE, Little Nightmares II.
>
> M0.7 fixes declared by director Manuel:
> - **M0.7.1** — `?clean=1` hides HUD / panels / bitácora
> - **M0.7.2** — `groundExt.applyFog = false` (kills the E8 "ice white" ground)
> - **M0.7.3** — Cascade ribbons upgraded to StandardMaterial + ALPHABLEND with
>   UV-scroll + secondary foam ring
> - **M0.7.4** — E8: drop 33 3D metropolis meshes; let `city-silhouette_001.jpg`
>   be the hero (gothic skyline + 5 warm-flicker faros)
> - **M0.7.5** — Avatar rim PointLight (warm, top-back)

## TL;DR

**M0.7 is a surgical pass that fixed the two most damaging E8 defects from
M0.5/M0.6 and got the camera out of the way of the HUD, but the slice is
NOT yet Planet-of-Lana / Trine-4 quality.** The metropolis reveal is now a
real cinematic frame (3/10 → 7/10) and no shot bleeds a narrative panel
anymore. **However:** the cascade is still a translucent ice-block, the
ground is still a 50–60 % macro carpet in every E2–E7 shot, the avatar
is still a 6-pixel smudge in 9/11 shots, and the painted mountain
backdrop that was M0.6's single biggest win has visibly **regressed** in
several E2 wide / fresh frames. Net: **0 / 11 PASS, 11 / 11 FAIL**, with
one **BORDERLINE (7/10)** on the E8 metropolis and one borderline
(6/10) on the E2 dolly.

> "M0.7 surgically excised the two worst M0.5/M0.6 E8 cancers (narrative
> bleed, white ice ground) and gave the city silhouette the room it
> needed to be the hero. The metropolis is now publisher-credible. But
> the cascade still reads as ice, the dirt carpet still reads as a
> macro of gravel, and the mountain backdrop in E2 lost warmth and
> detail. We need one more focused pass on the cascade material and
> the ground scale before the slice is shippable."

## Global observations (apply across the 11 shots)

1. **E8 is the single biggest win of M0.7.** The metropolis frame now
   actually feels like a Little Nightmares II / INSIDE ending shot: dark
   city silhouette, warm window lights, moon, isolated character, brown
   dirt. The narrative-bleed cancer is gone. If only the other 10 shots
   were at this level, the slice would be near-shippable.

2. **The cascade is technically upgraded but still does not read as
   water.** M0.7.3 added a second ribbon and a foam-ring cluster at the
   base, which is visible in `e2-cascada-dolly` and `e2-cascada-cornisa-right`
   as internal texture and a small bright cluster. But the outer shell of
   the cascade is still the same translucent pale-cyan slab with hard
   vertical edges. To a Planet-of-Lana / Trine-4 reviewer, this is
   still "ice-block with extra steps", not water. There is no white
   spray at the impact point, no mist plume rising from the base, no
   specular highlight on the stream, no refraction of the BG through
   the water. INSIDE uses the cascade as a hero element; here the
   cascade is the second weakest thing on screen (after the dirt carpet).

3. **The painted mountain backdrop has regressed in E2.** In M0.6 the
   `mountain-far_001.jpg` painted silhouette was the single best asset
   in the slice — layered peaks, warm-to-cool gradient, alpenglow, visible
   sun. In M0.7 the same mountain is still there, but the camera position
   in `e2-cascada-fresh` and `e2-cascada-wide` crops the painted detail
   and only a small dark-teal mass is visible in the upper-right corner.
   The sun is no longer visible in these frames and the sky is a flat
   pale cyan with no warm gradient. E3/E4/E6/E7 still show the mountain
   backdrop properly because the camera is further down the dolly, so
   the regression is concentrated in the E2 frames closest to the start.

4. **The ground plane is still a macro carpet.** This was M0.6 defect
   #2 and it is **unchanged** in M0.7. Every E2–E7 frame has the
   `ground-dirt-tile_001.jpg` filling 40–60 % of the lower half at
   extreme close range. The viewer reads it as a high-res photo of
   gravel, not as terrain. There is still no horizon haze blending
   the dirt into the sky/mountain.

5. **The cornisa is still a thin floating plank.** No contact shadow,
   no AO at the join with the ground, the same 0.2–0.4 m thickness as
   M0.5 / M0.6. The cornisa is not shippable as a Trine / Little
   Nightmares II element — it reads as a sticker on a beam.

6. **The avatar and instrument are still tiny.** In 9 of 11 shots the
   character occupies < 2 % of the frame, with no silhouette readability
   against the bright sky. The "rim light" M0.7.5 added is not
   verifiable at this scale. The instrument (when visible) is a small
   white blob in `e3-equilibrio` and `e5-vector`, not a companion.

7. **The "B" cluster of dots/marks** in the upper area of E2 frames
   (`e2-fresh`, `e2-cornisa-left`, `e2-cornisa-right`, `e2-wide`) is
   **still present** in M0.7. In M0.6 the audit flagged it as "B
   letters in the foreground read as UI/text". In M0.7 the cluster is
   smaller and looks more like dust particles, but it is still
   unexplained visual noise in the upper area of every E2 frame.

8. **No HUD bleed anywhere.** This is a real, scene-wide win. M0.5 had
   the narrative panel; M0.6 still had it in E8; M0.7 has it nowhere.
   The harness change to `?clean=1` is doing its job.

## Prioritized fix list for the next milestone

In order of how much damage each defect is doing to publisher-credibility:

1. **Cascade material — finish the water shader (M0.7.3 was the
   start, not the end).** The current StandardMaterial + ALPHABLEND
   ribbons are still a translucent slab. Planet of Lana / INSIDE use
   the cascade as a hero element. We need:
   - White spray particle plume at the base (inspiration: pSystem
     with 50–80 small white sprites, life 0.4–0.8 s, gravity -2)
   - Foam ring (the "secondary foam ring" from M0.7.3 is barely
     visible; needs to be a clear white torus with UV scroll, sitting
     at the water-meets-ground plane)
   - Mist particle system (alpha-blended billboard sprites, drifting
     upward, slow)
   - Specular highlight from the sun direction (Babylon `HighlightLayer`
     on the cascade mesh, or a Phong specular term)
   - Optional: refraction of the BG mountains through the lower half
     of the cascade (Babylon `RefractionTexture`)

2. **Ground plane — kill the macro carpet.** This is M0.6 defect #2
   and it is still the #1 visual issue in 10 of 11 shots. Options,
   in order of preference:
   - **Camera change (cheapest):** pull the camera up and back in
     `e2-fresh`, `e2-wide`, `e2-cornisa-left/right` so the dirt
     occupies < 30 % of the frame. Treat the dirt as terrain, not as
     a foreground close-up.
   - **Texture change:** switch to a lower-frequency, larger-scale
     dirt pattern, or apply a 4-octave noise that fades to the BG
     color at the seam.
   - **Material change:** add a horizon haze shader (radial alpha
     falloff at the far edge of the ground plane) so the dirt fades
     to the sky color at the BG seam. Trine 4 / Little Nightmares II
     use this trick.
   - **Color change:** replace the saturated orange-brown with a
     desaturated mauve / cool brown that recedes behind the cornisa.

3. **Mountain backdrop regression in E2 — restore the painted
   silhouette framing.** The painted mountain in `e2-fresh` and
   `e2-wide` is now cropped to a small dark mass. Either move the
   camera back, scale the mountain-far plane, or re-aim the camera
   to put the sun + alpenglow back in frame. The M0.6 mountain was
   the single best asset; we lost ground on it.

4. **Avatar + instrument readability — the M0.7.5 rim light needs a
   scaled-up subject.** Even with a perfect rim light, a 30-pixel
   character is unreadable. Options:
   - Scale the avatar up 1.5–2x (currently ~0.4 m, try 0.7–0.8 m).
   - Move the camera 30 % closer to the cornisa in the E2/E3 frames
     where the character is the subject.
   - Add a subtle dark silhouette fill on the back of the character
     so it reads as a black figure against the bright sky (INSIDE
     style).

5. **Cornisa contact shadow.** Still floating, no AO, no shadow. Add
   a soft circular shadow under the plank.

6. **Staircase geometry (E6/E7).** Still a stack of thin rectangles.
   Replace with real plank treads + riser gaps, or apply a stylized
   staircase texture with visible step edges.

7. **"B" cluster of dots in E2 upper area.** Still unexplained visual
   noise. Either remove (if debug) or replace with a deliberate
   atmospheric particle (dust, snow, leaves) that has a name in the
   asset list.

8. **Midground walls need side-key lighting.** `cliff-wall-v2` is on
   the surface but the walls are still flat PBR planes. Add a
   directional light from the sun direction + soft AO at the base
   so the stone reads as carved.
