## TL;DR

**M0.7.1 is a real, visible pass.** All 4 fixes land on screen. The
**cascade finish (M0.7.1.2)** is the single biggest win: a clear
white foam torus is now visible at the impact point in every cascade
shot, and the spray/mist amplification gives the cascade a real
"water hitting a pool" read instead of the M0.7 ice-block. The
**ground regrade (M0.7.1.1)** is a partial pass — the orange-brown
carpet is gone, the cool desaturated brown reads atmospheric, and
the texture is less repetitive — but the ground still occupies
~50% of the lower half in 8/11 shots. The **avatar scale 1.5x
(M0.7.1.3)** is unambiguously working: the cone is now clearly
readable in the E2 close-ups (`e2-fresh`, `e2-cornisa-left`,
`e2-wide`). The **cornisa contact shadow (M0.7.1.4)** is visible
in the E2 close-ups as a clear dark band under the cornisa and
the "floating sticker" read is reduced, but it is a flat bar
rather than a soft radial gradient. Net: **0 / 11 PASS, 7 / 11
BORDERLINE FAIL, 1 / 11 BORDERLINE PASS, 3 / 11 FAIL.** Average
M0.7 → M0.7.1: **5.00 → 5.73 (+0.73).**

> "M0.7.1 fixed the four things M0.7 left on the table. The cascade
> now has a real foam base, the ground no longer reads as 'orange
> gravel macro,' the avatar is a person instead of a smudge, and
> the cornisa finally touches the dirt. The slice is *moving*

> One more focused pass — M0.7.2 — should finish the cascade
> material, reduce the ground's frame share, and rebuild the

### Side effects of the M0.7.1 fixes (not requested, must be flagged)

1. **The cascade got BIGGER in some shots.** The foam torus + amplified
   spray add visible white pixels around the base, and the new mist
   particles spread beyond the cascade footprint. Net: the cascade
   occupies more frame area in M0.7.1 than in M0.7, especially in
   `e2-cornisa-right` (~40% of frame, was 30%) and `e3-equilibrio`
   (~30%, was 30%). This is not a regression in cascade quality (the
   foam is real) but it does make the cascade dominate the frame more.
   The producer should consider whether the cascade mesh itself needs
   a slight scale reduction, or whether the camera needs to pull back
   10–15% in the E2/E3 cascade frames.

2. **The mountain backdrop in `e2-fresh` and `e2-wide` is now a
   massive dark teal mass.** In M0.7 the mountain was "a small dark
   teal mass in the upper-right corner." In M0.7.1 the camera framing
   (or the ground's reduced scale) means the mountain now fills the
   right 50% of the frame in these two shots. This is not necessarily
   a regression — the painted detail (lighter teal at the top, darker
   at the bottom, snow patches) is more visible than in M0.7 — but
   the mountain now reads as a low-poly 3D mass rather than a
   layered painted silhouette. In `e2-dolly`, `e3`, `e4`, `e6`,
   `e7` the mountain backdrop is still beautifully painted (peaks,
   alpenglow, clouds, atmospheric haze, pine trees on `e6` and `e7`).

3. **A blue rectangle under the cornisa in `e2-wide`.** Not described
   in any fix. Could be a debug axis, a color-coded ground strip, or
   a misplaced shadow. Flag this for the producer.

4. **The "B cluster" of white particles in the upper area of E2 frames
   is still present, and may now be the amplified mist from M0.7.1.2.**
   In M0.7 the audit said "B cluster is still unexplained visual
   noise in the upper area of every E2 frame." In M0.7.1 the cluster
   is more visible because the mist was amplified (rate 110→180).
   In `e2-dolly` the particles look like dust motes catching the sun
   (Planet of Lana style ✓). In `e2-fresh` and `e2-cornisa-right` the
   particles look more like a swarm of noise. The producer should
   either bound the mist particle system to the cascade volume, or
   tune the alpha so the cluster looks intentional.

## Prioritized fix list for the next milestone (M0.7.2)

In order of how much damage each defect is doing to publisher-credibility:

1. **Cascade material — finish the water shader (M0.7.1.2 was 30%,
   we need 70% more).** The foam torus and amplified spray are real
   wins, but the cascade body is still a translucent pale-cyan slab.
   For Planet of Lana / INSIDE quality we still need:
   - **Vertical spray plume** at the impact point (a clear vertical
     column of white particles rising 1–2u above the base, with
     gravity -2 and life 0.4–0.8s)
   - **Mist particle system** (alpha-blended billboard sprites
     drifting upward from the base, slow, life 2–3s) — the current
     M0.7.1.2 mist looks like dust motes, not a mist plume
   - **Internal flow / refraction** in the cascade body (Babylon
     `RefractionTexture` or a custom UV-scroll on the existing
     StandardMaterial with a noise normal map)
   - **Specular highlight** from the sun direction (Babylon
     `HighlightLayer` on the cascade mesh, or a Phong specular term)
   - **Reduce cascade size** by 15–20% in the E2/E3 frames where it
     currently dominates (the foam/spray finish made it bigger, not
     smaller)
   - Verify with `shot-e3-equilibrio-fresh.png` — the cascade there
     should read as "waterfall hitting a pool," not as "translucent
     column with foam at the base"

2. **Ground plane — reduce frame share, not just desaturate.** M0.7.1.1
   fixed the color and the texture repetition, but the ground still
   occupies ~50% of the lower frame in 8/11 shots. Options, in order
   of preference:
   - **Camera change (cheapest):** pull the E2 cameras up+back 15–20%
     so the dirt occupies < 35% of the frame
   - **Horizon haze shader:** add a radial alpha falloff at the far
     edge of the ground plane so the dirt fades to the sky color at
     the BG seam (Trine 4 / Little Nightmares II use this)
   - **Texture swap:** replace `ground-dust_001.jpg` with a
     lower-frequency, larger-scale pattern (the current one is still
     too readable at close range)
   - Verify with `shot-e2-cascada-fresh.png` and `shot-e2-wide.png`:
     dirt should recede, not dominate

3. **Staircase geometry (E6/E7) — finally replace the stacked
   rectangles.** This is a M0.5 issue that has survived M0.6, M0.7,
   and M0.7.1. Options:
   - Replace with real plank treads + visible riser gaps
   - Apply a stylized staircase texture with visible step edges
   - Replace with a single ladder (vertical rungs) which is cheaper
     and reads as a Trine 4 element
   - Verify with `shot-e6-plano-fresh.png` and `shot-e7-estacion-fresh.png`:
     the staircase should read as climbable terrain, not as a
     vertical stack of thin boards

4. **"B cluster" white particles in E2 upper area — bound or
   remove.** Still present in `e2-fresh`, `e2-cornisa-right`,
   `e2-wide`. The M0.7.1.2 mist amplification made them more
   visible. Either:
   - Bound the mist particle system to a tight volume around the
     cascade so particles don't drift to the upper area
   - Replace the existing particle system with a deliberate
     "dust mote" effect with a clear name in the asset list
   - Remove entirely if the original was a debug effect
   - Verify with `shot-e2-cascada-fresh.png`: the upper area
     should be clean sky, mountain, or intentional atmospheric haze

5. **Blue debug rectangle under the cornisa in `e2-wide.png`** —
   investigate and remove. Not described in any fix, looks like a
   misplaced color-coded ground strip or a debug axis line.

6. **Midground walls need side-key lighting.** `cliff-wall-v2` is
   on the surface (visible in `e2-cornisa-right` and `e4`) but the
   walls are still flat PBR planes. Add a directional light from
   the sun direction + soft AO at the base so the stone reads as
   carved. This would lift the score of `e2-cornisa-left`,
   `e3-equilibrio`, `e4-referencia`, `e5-vector`, `e6-plano`,
   `e7-estacion` all at once.

7. **E5 cascade cropping.** The cascade is cropped on the right
   edge of `e5-vector-fresh.png`. Either move the cascade inward
   in the level, or pull the camera back. This is a single
   composition fix.

8. **Cornisa contact shadow — soften the edges.** M0.7.1.4 works,
   but the shadow is a uniform-opacity band rather than a soft
   radial gradient. Add an alpha falloff so the shadow feathers
   out at the edges (more Trine 4 / Little Nightmares II style).

9. **E8 metropolis — add more warm faros.** 5 faros are visible;
   for a "metropolis" the city should have 10–15 illuminated
   windows. This is a single-asset-list change (add more
   `city-silhouette_001.jpg` instances with window patterns, or
   add a particle system of small warm sprites).

## Recommended next milestone: **M0.7.2 (water + ground + staircase)**

**Do not jump to M0.8 yet.** M0.7.1 was a real improvement but
the cascade body, the ground's frame share, and the staircase
geometry are the three remaining M0.6 / M0.5 issues that are
still blocking publisher-credibility. A focused M0.7.2 pass
should:

1. **Finish the cascade water shader** (M0.7.2.1) — vertical spray
   plume, mist particle system (not dust motes), internal flow /
   refraction, specular highlight. Reduce cascade size 15–20% in
   the E2/E3 frames where it dominates.
2. **Reduce the ground's frame share** (M0.7.2.2) — either pull
   the E2 cameras up+back 15–20%, or add a horizon haze shader
   so the dirt fades to the sky color at the BG seam.
3. **Rebuild the staircase** (M0.7.2.3) — real plank treads or a
   single ladder, no more stacked thin rectangles.
4. **Bound the "B cluster"** (M0.7.2.4) — tighten the mist
   particle system volume or remove it.
5. **Remove the blue debug rectangle** under the cornisa in
   `e2-wide.png`.
6. **Add side-key lighting to the midground walls** so
   `cliff-wall-v2` reads as carved stone.

If M0.7.2 lands cleanly, then **M0.8** can pick up the cornisa
contact shadow softening, the E8 faro count, the E5 cascade
cropping fix, and the cornisa mid-air scenarios in E6/E7 —
the remaining cosmetic issues that didn't make it into the
M0.7.2 batch.
