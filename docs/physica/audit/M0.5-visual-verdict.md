# Visual Director — M0.5 verdict

> Audit of `docs/physica/screenshots/shot-*.png` against the four target
> references in `docs/physica/references/external/`. Brutally demanding.

## Reference targets

- **Trine 4** — rich 2.5D, layered depth, physical materials, painterly
  textures with real lighting, "lived-in" surfaces.
- **Planet of Lana** — painterly silhouettes, warm horizon, **single dominant
  sun**, **layered mountain silhouettes receding into haze**, small
  protagonist in a large world.
- **INSIDE** — silhouette readability, rim + side key lighting, controlled
  composition, no HUD.
- **Little Nightmares II** — atmospheric depth, **composed 3 layers** (FG
  subject / MG architecture / BG atmosphere), directed scale.

## Global observations (apply to all 8 shots)

These are the five things the M0.5 build is missing across the board. They
explain almost every individual defect listed below.

1. **The "painted mountain silhouettes" do not exist.** The commit
   `88e42d2` claims "mountain fade-out" and "warm horizon", but in every
   shot that should have BG mountains we instead see a **hard horizontal
   dark band** (a `MeshBuilder.CreateBox` rectangle) cutting across the
   frame at the horizon line. The painted silhouettes referenced in the
   notes are absent. The BG reads as a stage flat, not as receding peaks.
2. **The single dominant sun is missing from the hero shot.** `e2-cascada-
   fresh` is the cascade reveal, the most cinematic frame — and there is
   no sun, no god-ray, no glow. Only `e2-dolly`, `e3`, `e4`, `e5`, `e6`,
   `e7`, `e8` show a small white disc; even there it is a flat sprite
   without any warm corona or sky bloom.
3. **The lower 25–35 % of every frame is a dead flat navy rectangle.** No
   terrain texture, no water shimmer, no parallax layer, no "ocean below
   the cornisa". This is the biggest single quality-killer in the slice
   — every shot looks like a cut-scene pasted onto a 4:3 letterbox.
4. **The midground "architecture" reads as test geometry.** Dark
   rectangular slabs, no material wear, no moss, no paint. The Trine
   brief says "warm woods, mossy stone, golden magic — each material has
   visible wear". We have none of that.
5. **The INSTRUMENTO (the companion that "looks at things when the avatar
   looks") is not visible or not readable in any of the 8 shots.** It
   should always be near the avatar, articulated, with rim light. Instead
   the avatar stands alone on every cornisa.

## Per-scene verdicts

### shot-e2-cascada-fresh.png — FAIL
This is the cascade reveal, the single most important frame in the slice,
and it is the worst of the eight. The Planet of Lana target — "single
dominant sun, golden horizon, the cascade rising against a soft sky" — is
not even on the table.

- **Verdict: FAIL**
- **Top 3 visible defects:**
  1. **No sun.** A cascade reveal without a single dominant light source
     is not a cascade reveal. The frame is evenly lit.
  2. **Hard horizontal dark band at the horizon.** This is the missing
     painted mountain silhouette rendered as a `Box` mesh. The line where
     it meets the sky is razor sharp; the band itself has zero internal
     detail. Reads as a stage flat, not mountains.
  3. **Lower 35 % of frame is a flat navy rectangle.** No water, no
     terrain, no shading, no parallax — pure dead space. The cornisa
     floats over a void.
- **Specific correction:**
  - Add the dominant sun + god-ray + sky bloom that the Planet of Lana
    notes demand.
  - Replace the dark `Box` "mountain" with the actual painted silhouette
    planes (or extrude a real silhouette mesh). Make sure the silhouettes
    have a soft fade-out toward the horizon, not a hard edge.
  - Populate the lower 35 % with the sea: animated plane with foam,
    parallax color bands, foam particles where the cascade meets the
    water.

### shot-e2-cascada-dolly.png — FAIL
The dolly wide shot is compositionally broken: the playable scene is
compressed into a narrow horizontal strip in the middle, with massive
dead navy above and below. Inside the strip the framing is actually the
best of the eight — the warm sky, the small sun, the wispy clouds, the
cascade all do their job. But the camera is not set up for a wide
cinematic.

- **Verdict: FAIL**
- **Top 3 visible defects:**
  1. **Massive letterbox above and below the playable strip.** ~22 % top
     and ~25 % bottom is dead navy. The "dolly" intent is a wide vista,
     not a portrait window.
  2. **Visible "viewport rectangle" edges** at the right and bottom of
     the active scene strip — a faint seam where the gameplay region
     meets the void. The camera frustum is leaking.
  3. **Cascade reads as a featureless translucent pillar.** No water
     texture, no internal motion, no spray, no mist. It is a
     `MaterialPlugin.Translucent` slab.
- **Specific correction:**
  - The dolly state must pull the camera back (and possibly raise it) so
     the playable plane fills ~85 % of the 1280×720 frame. Adjust the
     FOV/distance in `physica-shot.mjs` until the cascade sits inside
     the frame with breathing room.
  - The cascade material needs noise + scroll on the alpha channel so
     the water reads as falling, not as a static pane.

### shot-e3-equilibrio-fresh.png — FAIL
The composition is correct in spirit — sun in the middle, character
left-of-center, cascade on the right — but the BG silhouette band is
back, the dark slab "walls" in the midground read as test geometry, and
the cornisa has nothing on it. There is no balance, no rhythm, no
"composed 3 layers" that Little Nightmares calls for.

- **Verdict: FAIL**
- **Top 3 visible defects:**
  1. **Hard dark band at the horizon** — same defect as e2-fresh. Painted
     mountains absent.
  2. **Three dark rectangular "wall" slabs** in the midground that
     obviously came from `MeshBuilder.CreateBox` with a default PBR
     material. No moss, no wear, no light, no story. They look like
     debug helpers.
  3. **The INSTRUMENTO is missing.** The notes demand it be visible
     "near the avatar, with intentional spacing" — here the avatar stands
     alone on an empty cornisa.
- **Specific correction:**
  - Same horizon fix as e2.
  - Replace the test-geometry walls with painted wood-stone slabs (or
     apply a real material) and add rim/key lighting consistent with
     the sun direction.
  - Place the INSTRUMENTO clearly within the FG cornisa — 0.4–0.8 m to
     the side, slightly behind, with a small float bob.

### shot-e4-referencia-fresh.png — FAIL
The sun is visible, the cascade is on the right, the character is
planted on a cornisa. The composition has the bones of something good.
But every surface is flat and every "wall" is a `Box`. The frame feels
like a level editor screenshot, not a Trine/Planet of Lana image.

- **Verdict: FAIL**
- **Top 3 visible defects:**
  1. **Cornisa is a featureless brown rectangle.** No wood grain, no
     plank seams, no wear. The moodboard shows the cornisa as the
     warmest, most-lit part of the frame; here it is the most ignored.
  2. **The midground walls are dark `Box` slabs** with uniform PBR
     shading and zero texture. No moss, no cracks, no color variation.
  3. **The sun has no corona, no god-ray, no sky bloom.** A flat white
     disc pasted on the gradient.
- **Specific correction:**
  - The cornisa material must be a painted plank (Trine-style wood with
     visible grain) lit by a key light from the sun direction.
  - Midground walls need either a stylized wood/stone texture or a real
     painted silhouette with parallax.
  - Sun must be a billboard quad with radial falloff and bloom, plus a
     god-ray / volumetric shaft for atmosphere.

### shot-e5-vector-fresh.png — FAIL
A side-scrolling puzzle composition with a sun in the middle, several
platforms, and a tiny avatar in the bottom third. The frame is mostly
empty. The visual interest is being carried entirely by the HUD pill at
the bottom — that is a sign the world itself is failing to do its job.

- **Verdict: FAIL**
- **Top 3 visible defects:**
  1. **The avatar is barely visible** — a tiny 6-pixel-tall blue smudge
     at the bottom. Not articulated, no rim light, no silhouette
     readability. INSIDE standards: **fail**.
  2. **The midground "walls" and platforms are flat dark `Box` slabs**
     with no material, no wear, no warmth. Reads as placeholder.
  3. **The lower 35 % is dead navy again.** No water, no terrain, no
     glow from the cascade. The frame feels amputated.
- **Specific correction:**
  - The avatar must occupy a clear fraction of the frame, with
     key+rim lighting, dark silhouette against a lit background.
  - Platforms/walls need stylized textures (plank, stone, moss).
  - The lower 35 % needs to be filled with the water plane.

### shot-e6-plano-fresh.png — FAIL
Closest of the eight to a "real" composition. The sun is in the right
place, the character is in the middle, the stairs structure on the left
provides vertical interest. But the stairs are stacked rectangles, the
bottom is dead navy, and the material story is missing.

- **Verdict: FAIL**
- **Top 3 visible defects:**
  1. **The "stairs" on the left are stacked `Box` rectangles.** No
     treads, no risers, no painted texture, no wear. Trine standards
     fail hard.
  2. **Midground walls are flat dark `Box` slabs**, identical in color
     and material to the stairs. No separation between FG and MG.
  3. **The lower 25 % is dead navy.** Same void as every other shot.
- **Specific correction:**
  - The stairs need real geometry (plank treads, riser gaps) or a
     painted stylized texture.
  - Light the FG stairs with a warmer key; keep the MG walls cooler and
     darker so depth reads.
  - Fill the lower 25 % with the water/terrain.

### shot-e7-estacion-fresh.png — FAIL
The frame is mostly empty. The "stairs" structure dominates the upper-
left quadrant but is a stack of rectangles. The character is a small
dark smudge. The lower 35 % is dead navy. The HUD pill is the most
polished thing on screen.

- **Verdict: FAIL**
- **Top 3 visible defects:**
  1. **The stairs are stacked `Box` rectangles** with no plank geometry
     and no texture.
  2. **The character is unreadable** — a few blue pixels against the
     cornisa, no silhouette, no rim, no key.
  3. **The lower 35 % is dead navy.** The cornisa floats over a void.
- **Specific correction:**
  - Same stairs fix as e6.
  - Add key+rim lighting to the avatar, or at minimum a darker
     silhouette against a lit cornisa.
  - Fill the lower 35 %.

### shot-e8-metropoli-teleport.png — FAIL (CRITICAL)
The metropolis reveal — the climax of the slice — is the worst frame of
the eight. The viewport is squashed to a small upper-right area, the
narrative text overlay ("Fís — Fundamentos", "Registro de iteración",
"Quietud activa mediante onda", …) bleeds into the scene, the cascade
barely peeks on the right, and the bottom half of the frame is a single
flat navy rectangle.

- **Verdict: FAIL (CRITICAL)**
- **Top 3 visible defects:**
  1. **Narrative text overlaps the scene in the upper-left.** The
     page-level narrative panel is rendered on top of the game canvas.
     This is a UI/scene integration bug — the page is in the wrong
     state, the narrative is leaking through, or the canvas is not
     getting the full viewport.
  2. **The active scene is a narrow strip in the upper portion of the
     frame.** The camera is way too close / too high / too zoomed-in for
     a "metropolis reveal" that should show the city vast in front of
     the protagonist.
  3. **The cascade is reduced to a thin slice** on the far right. The
     metropolis — the payoff of the entire slice — is unreadable.
- **Specific correction:**
  - The metropolis state in `physica-shot.mjs` must use a much wider
     FOV / lower camera and the narrative panel must be hidden before
     screenshot.
  - The frame must show the cornisa on the FG (bottom), the staircase,
     the midground metropolis, and the cascade on the BG — in clear
     depth layers.
  - Verify the screenshot script hides any debug/narrative UI before
     the capture (similar to how `#px-continue` is dismissed).

## Cross-cutting recommendations (priority order)

1. **Add the painted mountain silhouette planes to every scene.** A
   layered parallax of 3–5 painted silhouette planes, soft fade-out at
   the horizon, warm-to-cool gradient from FG to BG. Currently the BG
   is a hard-edged `Box`.
2. **Build the water/terrain plane** for the lower 25–35 % of every
   frame. Animated foam, parallax color bands, foam particles where the
   cascade meets it. Without this, every shot is amputated.
3. **Replace the `MeshBuilder.CreateBox` "walls" with proper styled
   geometry/materials** (planks, stones, moss). Trine-stylized but lit
   by real PBR.
4. **Make the sun a real light + billboard** with corona, bloom, and
   god-ray. Every exterior shot should have a single dominant light.
5. **The INSTRUMENTO must be visible in every FG cornisa** — not stuck
   to the avatar, but near, articulated, with its own rim light.
6. **Fix the camera state in `physica-shot.mjs`** for `e2-cascada-dolly`
   and `e8-metropoli-teleport` — both are using the wrong FOV/distance
   for a wide shot, and `e8` is not hiding the narrative panel.
7. **Avatar readability pass** — key + rim lighting on the avatar so
   the silhouette reads against any background, INSIDE-style.
