# Visual Director — M0.6 verdict (re-audit)

> Re-audit of `docs/physica/screenshots/shot-*.png` (11 shots, 7 scenes)
> after the **hybrid art pipeline** applied over the Physica slice.
> References: Planet of Lana, Trine 4, INSIDE, Little Nightmares II.

## TL;DR

**There is a real, visible quality jump vs. M0.5.** The painted mountain
backdrop and the dirt ground plane fix the two biggest M0.5 defects
(mountain hard-Box and navy void). The slice now has actual horizon depth,
a visible sun, and a continuous "world" under the cornisa. **But three
new critical issues appear in M0.6, and the slice is still not commercial
Planet-of-Lana / Trine 4 quality.** Verdict: **0 / 11 PASS, 11 / 11 FAIL**
(one borderline — `shot-e2-cascada-dolly` — at 7/10, the rest ≤ 6/10).

| What M0.5 said | What M0.6 actually did | Net |
|---|---|---|
| Hard Box mountain band at horizon | **`mountain-far_001.jpg` painted silhouette + alpenglow sky** | ✅ solved |
| Lower 25-35% dead navy void | **`ground-dirt-tile_001.jpg` dirt plane** | ⚠️ solved the void, but the texture is now **a macro carpet that dominates the FG** |
| Dark Box midground "walls" | **`cliff-wall-v2_001.jpg` stone walls** | ⚠️ partial — texture is on, but the slabs still read as flat 2D rectangles; no PBR variation |
| Cascade = static translucent pillar | unchanged | ❌ still a translucent ice-block, not water |
| e8 narrative-text bleeding through | **NOT fixed** | ❌ regression — `Physica — Fundamentos` panel still painted over the upper-right of the frame |

## Global observations (apply to almost all 11 shots)

1. **The painted mountain backdrop is the single biggest win in M0.6.**
   `mountain-far_001.jpg` reads as a real Planet-of-Lana / Trine-4
   distance: layered peaks, soft fade, warm-to-cool gradient, atmospheric
   haze. This is the first time the slice has a believable world horizon.
   Credit where due: the BG is now in the right neighborhood.

2. **The dirt ground plane solved the navy void, but introduced a new
   "macro carpet" problem.** `ground-dirt-tile_001.jpg` is clearly a
   valid tileable dirt/gravel texture, but in the 11 shots it fills
   **40–60 % of the frame at extreme close range**, so the viewer reads
   it as a high-resolution close-up of gravel — not as terrain. The
   perspective also makes the orange-brown plane fight the cooler sky,
   and there is no horizon "blend" between ground and mountain backdrop:
   the seam where the dirt meets the sky is razor-sharp in most shots.

3. **The cascade is still a translucent ice-block.** Every shot that
   shows the cascade (`e2-fresh`, `e2-dolly`, `e2-cornisa-right`, `e2-wide`,
   `e3-equilibrio`, `e4-referencia`) shows it as a vertical pale-blue
   slab with hard edges, scroll-tiled noise, and a translucent alpha
   outline. It reads as a piece of ice / a window pane, not as falling
   water. No spray, no foam where it meets the ground, no mist, no
   specular, no refraction. INSIDE and Planet of Lana use the cascade
   as a hero element; here it is the weakest object on screen.

4. **The cornisa is a thin floating plank.** The new
   `plank-top-clean_001.jpg` wood texture is more "plank" than the old
   canvas-painted slab, but the cornisa is still 0.2–0.4 m thick and
   it floats over the dirt ground with no shadow and no contact shadow
   at the join. The Trine reference demands a heavy, lived-in plank with
   visible edge wear; here it is a sticker on a beam.

5. **The midground "walls" (cliff-wall-v2) have texture but no mass.**
   `cliff-wall-v2_001.jpg` is visible — you can see rock patterns and
   some mossy variation — but the slabs are still flat PBR planes, the
   same width as before, with default PBR shading. They read as
   **posters of rock walls**, not as actual stone with depth. Trine and
   Little Nightmares II use parallax + side-key lighting to make the
   stone feel carved; here the stone feels printed on a card.

6. **The avatar and instrument are still tiny.** In 9 of 11 shots the
   character occupies < 2 % of the frame, with no rim light, no
   silhouette readability against the bright BG, and the companion
   "instrumento" is either absent or a 6-pixel smudge. INSIDE standards:
   the avatar is a black silhouette against a lit world; here the
   avatar is a blue smudge on a brown plank.

7. **e8 is the worst regression of the M0.6 batch.** The narrative panel
   ("Physica — Fundamentos — Registro de llegada, Quietud activa …")
   is still painted on top of the upper-right corner of the scene. The
   M0.5 audit explicitly flagged this as CRITICAL and the fix did not
   land. The city silhouette also reads as a row of white/light
   rectangles against the mountains — the city-silhouette_001.jpg
   detail (illuminated windows, gothic profiles) is not readable at the
   current camera distance and scale. The ground in e8 also changes
   material — it is now white/ice, not brown dirt — which is an
   unexplained inconsistency with the other 10 shots.

## Prioritized fix list (in order)

If the slice has one more pass before showing it to a publisher-quality
reviewer, this is the order in which to attack the remaining defects.

1. **Hide the narrative panel before screenshot in E8.** This is the
   single most damaging issue. The `Physica — Fundamentos` text overlay
   must be `display: none` (or the page must be in the right state)
   when `physica-shot.mjs` captures the metropolis frame. The M0.5
   audit flagged this as CRITICAL and it has not been addressed.

2. **Re-scale or re-texture the ground plane.** The
   `ground-dirt-tile_001.jpg` plane is dominating every frame as a
   macro carpet. Options, in order of preference:
   - Move the camera up and back so the dirt occupies < 25 % of the
     frame (treat it as terrain, not a foreground close-up).
   - Switch to a lower-frequency / larger-scale dirt pattern, or apply
     a dark vignette + horizon haze so the dirt fades to the BG color
     at the seam.
   - Replace the dirt with a quieter, more atmospheric color band
     (mauve / desaturated brown) that recedes behind the cornisa.

3. **Fix the cascade material.** Replace the translucent pale-blue
   slab with a real water shader: scrolling noise on alpha, white
   foam at the impact point, mist particle plume at the base, rim
   light from the sun, a subtle god-ray passing through it. The
   cascade is the hero element of E2 and it is currently the weakest
   thing on screen.

4. **Re-grade e8 city silhouette and ground.** The metropolis should
   read as a real city: illuminated windows, gothic towers, a
   layered skyline with FG / MG / BG depth. The current
   `city-silhouette_001.jpg` either needs to be rendered at a larger
   scale, a darker time-of-day, or a different camera distance so the
   buildings read. The ground should match the other shots (brown
   dirt) or be a deliberate snowy/ice scene with the materials
   consistent.

5. **Avatar + instrument readability pass.** The character is a small
   blue smudge in 9 of 11 shots. Add a rim light from the sun
   direction and a darker silhouette fill so the character reads
   against the bright sky (INSIDE standard). The instrument should
   be near the avatar (0.4–0.8 m), slightly behind, with a small
   float bob — not floating 5 m away as a small orb.

6. **Midground walls need depth, not just texture.** `cliff-wall-v2`
   is on the surface but the walls are still flat PBR planes. Add
   side-key lighting from the sun direction, soft AO at the base
   where the wall meets the ground, and a slight parallax so the
   slabs feel like they have mass. Trine / Little Nightmares II
   use the side-key to make stone feel carved.

7. **Cornisa contact shadow.** The cornisa floats over the dirt with
   no shadow and no contact AO. Add a soft circular shadow under it
   so the plank feels grounded.

8. **Staircase geometry (E6/E7).** The staircase is still a stack of
   thin rectangles. Replace with real plank treads + riser gaps, or
   apply a stylized staircase texture with visible step edges.

## Final verdict

**The slice is NOT ready to show a Planet-of-Lana / Trine-4 publisher.**

There is a **genuine, visible step up** from M0.5 → M0.6 on the
background horizon (mountain-far + sky + sun), the midground stone
texture, and the elimination of the navy void. The BG of the slice
is now in the right neighborhood of the reference. **But the FG
and the hero element (cascade) are still wrong**, and **e8 has a
regression** on the most damaging M0.5 issue (narrative bleed).

If forced to summarize in one sentence for a producer:

> *"M0.6 fixed the two biggest M0.5 defects — the painted mountain
> silhouette and the navy void — and the world now has a real horizon
> and a visible sun. But the cascade still reads as ice, the ground
> reads as a macro of carpet, and the metropolis reveal still bleeds
> the narrative panel. We need one more focused pass on those three
> things before this is shippable."*

### What's working
- Painted mountain backdrop (mountain-far_001.jpg) — Planet-of-Lana
  quality, the single best asset in M0.6.
- Sun corona + sky gradient — coherent, warm, atmospheric.
- Cliff-wall-v2 midground texture — visible moss/rock variation.
- Elimination of the navy void — every frame has a "world" under the
  cornisa now.

### What is not working
- Cascade = translucent ice pillar (M0.5 issue, not addressed).
- Ground plane = macro carpet dominating 40-60 % of every frame
  (new M0.6 issue, replacement of M0.5 navy void).
- E8 narrative panel still bleeding through (M0.5 CRITICAL issue,
  regression — not addressed).
- E8 city silhouette = row of rectangles, no readable architecture.
- E8 ground is white/ice (inconsistency with the other 10 shots).
- Cornisa = thin floating plank (M0.5 issue, partially addressed).
- Avatar + instrument = small smudges in 9 / 11 shots (M0.5 issue,
  not addressed).
- Midground walls have texture but no mass / no side-key lighting
  (M0.5 issue, partially addressed).

### Recommended next milestone
**M0.7 should target the three CRITICAL items in the prioritized fix
list (narrative bleed, cascade material, ground plane) before any
further scene tuning.** Those three are the difference between "this
is a level editor with a beautiful sky" and "this is a slice that a
publisher of Planet-of-Lana quality would look at twice".
