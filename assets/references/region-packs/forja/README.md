# Region Pack — Forja

Status: prepared for A1 review.

## Concept
A production space where electrical power becomes useful heat and work. It should feel heavy, hot and demanding on the network without becoming a magical forge or lava cavern.

## Hero landmarks
Primary electrical load/furnace, thick conductors, coordinated protection, temperature evidence, work path and material stock. The load/protection assembly is a hero-reference candidate.

## Authored focus
Make overload and recovery physical: heat, contact/protection behavior, sound, conductor scale and service consequence. Keep safe circulation and interaction points readable.

## Canonical shots
`forge-core`, `forge-terraces-overview`.

## Concept-generation prompt seed
"Ohmdal electrically powered forge integrated into ancient pale stone utility architecture, heavy copper conductors on ceramic insulators, dark steel furnace/load assembly, visible protection and thermal evidence, practical industrial workspace, warm amber heat localized to the active load, no magical fire, no lava cavern, no random sparks, grounded educational technical fantasy."

## Avoid
Fantasy blacksmith clichés, constant spark shower, oversized molten environment, red UI-as-world-state.
