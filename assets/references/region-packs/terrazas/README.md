# Region Pack — Terrazas

Status: prepared for A1 review.

## Concept
Stepped stone water/agricultural terraces whose irrigation depends on powered pumps/gates. More open, calm and water-oriented than the Forja so the service trade-off is spatially obvious.

## Landmarks
Stepped channels, pump/valve stations, visible distribution conductors, dry/wet or low/high-service states, sightline back toward the industrial demand where possible.

## Authored focus
Show propagation: network decisions change which terraces receive water. The player should read service consequence at a distance before consulting HUD/text.

## Canonical shots
`terraces-irrigation`, `forge-terraces-overview`.

## Concept-generation prompt seed
"Ancient Ohmdal powered irrigation terraces, pale eroded stone steps, narrow water channels, compact electric pumps and valves, aged copper conductors on ceramic supports, visibly different irrigated and dry service states, warm afternoon, restrained technical fantasy, open readable traversal, no generic lush fantasy farm, no glowing water."

## Avoid
Decorative farm scene disconnected from electricity, magical irrigation, excessive vegetation hiding technical routing.
