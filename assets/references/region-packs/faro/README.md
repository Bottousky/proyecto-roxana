# Region Pack — Faro / Lago

Status: prepared for A1 review.

## Concept
A remote, austere technical lighthouse/measurement landmark over the lake. After the density of Castillo and Forja, the space should feel quieter, more precise and contemplative while remaining visibly tied to the network.

## Hero landmarks
Lighthouse mechanism/light, calibration panel, lake reference, outgoing/return conductors and a clear observation/calibration position. The mechanism/calibration assembly is a hero-reference candidate.

## Current gameplay truth
The accepted greybox uses DC calibration and stable samples. Do not imply RC/transient pulse mechanics until that content is ratified.

## Authored focus
Make calibration consequences visible in the light/mechanism/world state without turning the Faro into a magical beam weapon.

## Canonical shots
`lighthouse-approach`, `lighthouse-lake-wide`.

## Concept-generation prompt seed
"Remote Ohmdal technical lighthouse beside a still lake, pale stone tower integrated with exposed but orderly copper conductors and ceramic insulators, analog calibration mechanism, instrument glass, restrained practical beacon, warm late-afternoon dusty atmosphere, grounded technical fantasy, quiet readable space, no magical laser, no cyberpunk neon, no unexplained pulse effects."

## Avoid
Coastal pirate lighthouse clichés, magical lighthouse beam, visual timing language that suggests unimplemented RC behavior.
